Research focus

Multi-physics · Multi-phase · Multi-scale

Research Professor at the Department of Chemical Engineering, Hankyong National University, and member of the Center of Sustainable Process Engineering (CoSPE). Co-founder & advisor at CFDWAYS LLC.

🌊

Computational Fluid Dynamics of Chemical Processes

Eulerian & VOF multiphase CFD of fluidized beds, bubble columns, molten-metal reactors, cyclones, reformers and CVD reactors — validated against experiments and scaled from lab to industry.

🧠

Physics-Informed Neural Networks

First-principles laws + deep learning (TensorFlow 2) for reactor model solution, parameter identification and optimization.

⚡

CO₂-free Hydrogen

Methane pyrolysis in molten-metal bubble column reactors — hydrodynamics, kinetics and scale-up.

♻️

CO₂ Methanation

Fixed-bed and fluidized-bed reactor design for carbon capture & utilization, from kinetics to optimal design.

🏭

Industry-scale Process Design

18+ funded projects with NRF Korea, KIER, Hyosung, GS E&C and semiconductor partners — steam-methane reformers, carbon-fiber lines, offshore separators, slurry hydrocracking and SiC epitaxy.
