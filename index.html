About

A researcher of flows, reactors, and the models that explain them.

SN

Son Ich Ngo (Ngô Ích Sơn / 노익선) is a Principal Researcher at the Center of Sustainable Process Engineering (CoSPE), Department of Chemical Engineering, Hankyong National University, Korea.

His work spans computational fluid dynamics of multiphase systems — bubble columns, fluidized beds, and molten-metal reactors — applied to hydrogen production, CO2 methanation, biomass gasification, and fluorinated-gas abatement. Recent research combines CFD with machine learning, including physics-informed neural networks for chemical reactor modeling.

Research

Interests & expertise.

Multiphase CFD

Eulerian and volume-of-fluid simulation of bubble columns, fluidized beds, and gas–liquid–solid reactors, from lab to industrial scale.

AI for CFD

Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) and machine-learning surrogates for parameter identification and reactor model solution.

Hydrogen Production

Methane pyrolysis in molten-metal bubble column reactors, steam methane reforming, and CO2-free hydrogen pathways.

CO2 Methanation

Fixed-bed and fluidized-bed reactor design, reaction kinetics, and heat-transfer optimization for power-to-gas systems.

Biomass & Energy

Dual circulating fluidized-bed gasification models and thermochemical conversion of biomass and waste plastics.

Environmental Processes

Destruction of fluorinated gases (SF6, NF3, CF4) in molten-metal reactors and offshore oil–water separation.
